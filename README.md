This  is a simple LSTM time series model built for my Data Science course. I built it to take in solar data from a dataset (source: Kaggle) and use it to predict the future levels of usage.
The program will then estimate a cost and what the cost could look like for the future if the trend continues. I intend to expand this simple course project into an app that can help people 
calculate energy bills based on their past usage and how much they can save if they use or conserve energy during certain hours.
